Privacy Policy

Google API Services Compliance
Hera Player's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

Introduction

At Hera Player, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when using the application.

Local Media Access

Hera Player accesses media files stored on your device solely to provide playback, library management, playlists, and related features. Media scanning and indexing occur locally on your device.

Google Drive Integration

When you choose to connect Google Drive, Hera Player may access files and folders that you explicitly select for synchronization, backup, or sharing.

Community Features (Hera Hub)

  • Message content
  • Random anonymous alias
  • Random installation UUID

No IMEI, MAC Address, serial number, or hardware-bound identifiers are collected.

Shared Cloud Content

For file sharing features, the following information may be stored:

  • File identifier
  • File title
  • File type
  • Associated Google account email address

Analytics & Diagnostics

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may collect anonymous diagnostic information including app version, crash reports, performance metrics, device model, and operating system version.

Advertising

Google AdMob may use the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) for advertising attribution, measurement, and personalization where permitted by user settings.

Biometric Authentication

If enabled, biometric authentication is processed entirely by your device operating system. Hera Player never stores or receives biometric data.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to help protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as required to provide services, improve functionality, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Children's Privacy

Hera Player is not intended for children under 13 years of age and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected by changing the Last Updated date.
