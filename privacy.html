# Hera Player Privacy Policy **Last Updated:** June 23, 2026 At Hera Player, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how Hera Player collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application. ## Information We Collect ### Local Media Access Hera Player accesses media files stored on your device solely to provide media playback, library management, playlist creation, and related features. Media scanning and indexing are performed locally on your device. ### Notifications If you enable notifications, Hera Player may send updates, playback controls, and important service-related notifications. ### Google Drive Integration When you choose to connect your Google account for cloud backup, synchronization, or file sharing, Hera Player may access files and folders that you explicitly select through Google Drive. Hera Player's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ### Community Features (Hera Hub) When posting messages within Hera Hub, the following information may be stored: * Message content * Randomly generated anonymous display alias * Randomly generated installation identifier (UUID) No hardware-based identifiers such as IMEI, MAC Address, or serial numbers are collected. ### Shared Cloud Content When using file-sharing features, the following information may be stored to manage shared links: * File identifier * File title * File type * Google account email associated with the shared content Users may remove shared content through the application whenever supported. ### Analytics and Diagnostics Hera Player uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability, performance, and user experience. These services may collect anonymous diagnostic information, including: * App version * Device model * Operating system version * Crash reports * Performance metrics ### Advertising Hera Player may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Advertising services may use the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) to provide ad attribution, measurement, and personalized advertising where permitted by your device settings. Users may manage advertising preferences through their device settings. ## Biometric Authentication If biometric authentication is enabled, fingerprint or face recognition data is processed entirely by your device's operating system. Hera Player never receives, stores, or transmits biometric data. ## Data Security We implement industry-standard security measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. ## Data Retention Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain functionality, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Users may request deletion of eligible cloud-stored content through available application controls. ## Children's Privacy Hera Player is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## Your Rights Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information. ## Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be reflected by changing the "Last Updated" date above. ## Contact Us For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact: Email: [support@heraplayer.com](mailto:support@heraplayer.com) By using Hera Player, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this Privacy Policy.
