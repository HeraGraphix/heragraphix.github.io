Hera Keyboard

Privacy Policy & Terms

Last Updated: March 2026

🛡️ Strict Offline-First & Privacy Guarantee:
Hera Keyboard is designed with a strict offline-first architecture. We do NOT collect, log, track, or transmit your keystrokes, personal messages, passwords, or credit card information to any remote servers.

1. Introduction & Ownership

Hera Keyboard is developed by HMSH Herath (Harshana Herath) / Hera Graphix. All rights, user interfaces, branding, layouts, and software components are proprietary and protected under intellectual property laws. We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring your data remains solely on your personal device.

2. Data Collection & Processing

Hera Keyboard does not operate user tracking servers or remote analytics for keyboard input:

  • Keystrokes & Text Input: All Sinhala Wijesekara, Singlish phonetic transliteration, and English typing calculations occur 100% locally on your device's memory.
  • Personal Dictionary & History: Saved user words, custom shortcuts, and clipboard history are saved strictly within local device storage and are never uploaded.
  • No Account Required: Hera Keyboard does not require user registration or profile creation.

3. Permissions & Usage

  • Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO): Used solely when the user manually initiates Voice Typing. Real-time speech recognition is handled securely by the device's built-in Android speech engine. Hera Keyboard does not record background audio or store voice logs.

4. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

If advertisements (e.g., Google AdMob) or in-app billing services are incorporated, those services may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic data in strict accordance with their respective privacy policies. No personal typing data is ever shared with third-party advertisers.
